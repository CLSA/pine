Reassign Respondents

This utility allows you to swap the responses between two respondents. This is typically used when an interview was performed under the wrong respondent. In order to proceed you must provide the questionnaire and two respondent records (by participant UID or token) that already exist in the selected questionnaire. The order of the respondents does not matter. The start, end and export datetimes as well as all responses and email records will be swapped.
PLEASE NOTE: The respondent token and attributes will not be swapped! If you wish to update the attribute values you must do so after the reassignment is complete.
